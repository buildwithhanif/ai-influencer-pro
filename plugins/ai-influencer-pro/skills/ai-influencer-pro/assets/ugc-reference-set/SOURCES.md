# The ten reference screenshots

Frames from real TikTok videos, found by searching "ugc" / "ugc creator" across US, GB and ID,
then scored with `scripts/score-frames.py` and picked for variety of person and of room.

They are a **look reference**, not face references: what a real creator's room, framing and light
actually look like. Nobody here is copied, and every cast member generated from them must be a new
person.

## A limitation worth knowing

The pool is overwhelmingly women in their twenties and thirties, because the people making content
*about* being a UGC creator are overwhelmingly women in their twenties and thirties. Across 105
candidates there were six usable men and nobody over about forty.

So the reference set demonstrates the framing, the rooms and the texture, but it does **not**
demonstrate an older face. The cast list still runs 18 to 68 and ChatGPT will extrapolate, but check
the older rows harder than the rest: that is where it has the least to copy and the most room to
invent a stock photo.

Do not republish these frames. They are working material, and the people in them are real.
