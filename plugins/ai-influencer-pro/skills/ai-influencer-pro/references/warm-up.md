# Warm-up and account operations

Everything here is operational practice, not platform gospel. Platforms change their heuristics without
telling anyone. The pattern below is what has kept fresh character accounts out of the "new account, low
trust" bucket in 2026, and it matches what f*ckgrowth, Kritarth and the Dagestan copycats do without
writing it down.

## Day 0 (before any post)
- One account per character per platform. Same handle everywhere you can get it. Bio in the character's
  voice, not "AI content creator". Disclose AI in the bio in one word: "AI" or "(AI)". Mbok Geni's bio
  literally says "AI (Asli Indramayu)" and nobody cares; hiding it is what people punish.
- Profile photo = the face crop from the character sheet. Not the full sheet.
- Real phone, real SIM, real Wi-Fi for the first two weeks. Phone farms are a later problem.
- No link in bio on day 0. Add it after post 10.

## Days 1-4: behave like a person
- 20-30 minutes a day scrolling the niche you will post in. Like generously, comment in the character's
  voice on 5-10 posts a day, follow 10-20 accounts in the lane. Save posts. Watch full videos.
- Do not post yet. The feed needs to learn what you are before it learns what you make.

## Days 5-30: post like a series
- 1 episode a day, same time slot, for 20 posts before you judge anything. Feijen's line holds:
  "Most people post eight, see flat numbers, and walk away one week before things start moving."
- Every post: PART number in the caption, the case number on the frame, the outro card with the handle.
  This is what converts a viewer into a follower. A one-off clip cannot.
- Reply to every comment in character for the first hour. Pin one comment that asks the audience
  something they can answer ("should he escalate to a PIP?").
- Post the same episode to TikTok, Reels and Shorts, but post natively. No watermark cross-posting.

## What to measure (and what to ignore)
- Follows per 1,000 views. That is the only number that tells you the character is working as a series.
  Mbok Geni: 11.6M views, 1,402 followers as of 20 Sep 2026 -> ~0.12 per 1,000. Views are fine, the
  outro card is doing nothing. Fix the CTA, not the content.
- Completion rate on the first 3 seconds. If it is below 60 percent the hook is a talking still, not an
  event. Put the event first.
- Ignore likes. Ignore your follower count for the first 30 days.

## AI labels and disclosure
- TikTok: label every post as AI-generated in the post settings. TikTok has said the label is not a
  negative ranking signal, and it has said that unlabeled content that gets flagged later loses reach
  while it is reviewed. Label it yourself.
- Instagram: "Made with AI" label on Reels. Same logic.
- Never say "same person btw" about a synthetic person. Never testimonial a product the character
  "used". The character can carry the product as a prop or a running joke; the character cannot vouch.

## Escalation ladder when something breaks
1. Views fall off a cliff on post N with nothing else changed: stop posting for 48 hours, keep engaging,
   resume with a different opening event.
2. Videos get "not eligible for recommendation": appeal once, then treat that clip as dead and move on.
3. Account restricted: do not create a second account from the same device that day. Wait it out.
